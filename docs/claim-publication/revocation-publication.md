# Revocation publication policy

Current valid authority means an active organization, active exact-profile management grant, and at least one active owner, manager, or staff membership. A historical approved claim alone is insufficient.

Revocation immediately denies new edits. The public projection then returns no managed profile and no business responses; cached copies expire within the bounded public API window. The Contractor page removes the currently-managed label and owner layer without changing DBPR evidence. Profile data, response revisions, claims, and audit history remain internal for staff review and may be projected again only after legitimate authority is re-established. There is no silent transfer or fuzzy identity merge.
