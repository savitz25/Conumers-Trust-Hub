# Claimed-language standard

Last reviewed: 2026-09-07.

Canonical meaning: “Claiming a profile confirms management access for an authorized business representative. It does not change public evidence, rankings, source status, Trust Hub research, or create an endorsement.”

Allowed labels: “Claimed profile,” “Profile managed by an authorized representative,” “Business-supplied information,” and “management access verified” when the context is access rather than quality.

Do not use “verified owner,” “verified business,” “approved company,” “certified business,” “trusted business,” or “claimed and verified.” The claim CTA may say “Claim this profile” when adjacent copy states the independence boundary. Business responses must be labeled business-supplied; support and email must describe authority review without suggesting a quality certification.
