# Arizona movers: no statewide license roster

ATH-AZ-001. Entity growth from an Arizona mover roster is **not available**.

## Official statement

Attorney General Kris Mayes and BBB, 2025-07-07:

> Arizona does not have a registration law or a professional licensing requirement for movers.

https://www.azag.gov/press-release/attorney-general-mayes-and-bbb-warn-moving-scams-arizona-0

## What Arizona does have

A.R.S. §§ **44-1611 to 44-1616** — hostage-load consumer protection for **intrastate** moves. Not a license. Not a company list.

DPS consumer path published in that PSA: **602-223-2212** / **602-223-5000**. The URL `https://www.azdps.gov/services/public/moving` returned HTTP **404** on 2026-09-04.

Interstate moves: FMCSA / USDOT. Confirm on the federal side. USDOT ≠ interstate authority by itself.
