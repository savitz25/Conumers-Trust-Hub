# Business Operations

`/admin/operations/businesses` is an internal, `ADMIN_VIEW`-protected lifecycle read model. It is not a second Business Manager and provides no staff impersonation or hidden Layer B editing.

Organizations remain account containers. Managed profiles remain exact specialist identities: mover/USDOT, NMLS institution, insurance legal insurer/NAIC, senior provider class plus CCN, contractor credential, and investment firm CRD. Organization status is never presented as regulatory status.

The list and detail routes combine authoritative organizations, memberships, claims, management grants, Layer B records, issues, replies, and domain audit history. Claim changes remain in Claim Operations. Staff cannot use this module to modify teams, evidence, responses, or public business fields.

Business-supplied profile completeness includes only description, website/public phone, services, and hours currently supported in Layer B. It excludes Layer A regulatory evidence and is not a Trust Score, verification, quality, safety, or ranking measure. Business activity and campaign participation have no ranking effect.

Privacy boundary: aggregate/internal business lifecycle data does not expose consumer Saves, consumer Watches, Projects, notes, raw Search questions, tokens, or shortlist membership. Queue views minimize business-user identity data.
