# Employee and agency exit

For a departing employee, terminated agency, job change, unreachable owner, personal-email claimant, or same-day removal request: independently verify current company authority; revoke the old exact grant/access; preserve audit history and sessions as applicable; approve a fresh claim only after the normal standard; then invite current staff with the least privilege.

Do not transfer ownership based on a phone assertion or mutate an account's email. Lost-email recovery requires a new authenticated account, fresh independent authority verification, review of old grants/sessions, revocation where appropriate, and an audit record.

There is no V1 ownership-transfer UI or secure document upload. Coordinate callbacks through pre-existing public contacts.
