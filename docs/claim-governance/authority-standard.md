# Claim authority standard

## Public-safe summary

Claiming requires verification of authority. Knowing a license or other public identifier is insufficient, and control of a company-domain email alone is insufficient. Ask Trust Hub may request additional verification or decline a claim when authority cannot be established. Claiming never changes public evidence, ranking, source status, or research ordering.

## Network rules

V1 is human-reviewed. Approval requires one independently sourced authority signal plus one validated control/contact signal, no unresolved conflict, a source note, an internal rationale, and a bounded claimant message. Public facts, claimant assertions, free email, business cards, LinkedIn, screenshots, and claimant-supplied contact channels are weak alone. Company-domain control is supporting evidence, never authority by itself.

Third-party operators need authorization from an independently verified officer or equivalent authority and receive the least-privileged team role. An agency's assertion cannot verify itself. An unresolved competing claim or active grant blocks approval until staff resolves authority.

## Florida Contractor V1

Accepted independent authority includes a current Florida corporate officer/authorized-person match, an applicable current DBPR qualifier/officer relationship, confirmation by a verified officer, or existing verified organization authority. Pair it with authenticated company-domain control, a callback through a contact published before the claim, or another independently sourced control channel. A qualifier who is not an owner must have verified authority for profile management.

Free-email users are not rejected automatically. They need stronger independent evidence, normally an authoritative-person match plus a pre-existing public-channel callback or verified-officer authorization. `owner + license number + Gmail` is insufficient.

Other Hub classes use the common rule; specialist-specific refinements remain required before unattended scale. No auto-approval is permitted.
