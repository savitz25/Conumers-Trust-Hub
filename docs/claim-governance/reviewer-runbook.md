# Reviewer runbook

1. Confirm the exact Hub profile, identifier, source system, class, and current specialist eligibility.
2. Review claimant email, free-email status, relationship attestation, organization context, other claims, and active grants.
3. Check sources independently; do not use claimant-supplied contact details as independent verification.
4. Select only evidence actually checked. Record source/reference detail without copying private documents or unnecessary PII.
5. Record an internal rationale separately from the neutral claimant-facing message.
6. Approve only when the server reports the standard met. A domain or credential alone never qualifies.
7. Use needs information when a safe independent check remains possible. Do not request secrets, identity documents, bank data, or sensitive corporate files by ordinary email; no secure upload exists in V1.
8. Reject neutrally when authority cannot be established or the relationship is ineligible. Do not allege fraud without an established incident process.
9. Treat an unresolved competing claim or active grant as blocking. Direct legitimate additional staff toward organization invitations.

Internal target: first action within 48 business hours for invited Contractor claims. Waiting on claimant pauses that target.
