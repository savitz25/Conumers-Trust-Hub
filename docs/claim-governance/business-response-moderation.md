# Business response moderation

Responses are business-supplied and never Trust Hub endorsement. Plain text is authoritative; raw HTML is not published. Human moderation is required.

Reject or request redaction for consumer/private-person names, home addresses, personal email or phone, health or financial data, government identifiers, private document content, passwords/tokens, threats, harassment, discriminatory content, doxxing, unsupported criminal accusations, character attacks, unsafe allegations, irrelevant promotion, or unsupported external links.

Neutral factual timelines, remediation, management changes, policy explanations, source-backed correction context, disagreement stated neutrally, resolution facts, and operational explanations may be publishable. Approval means publication-policy compliance, not factual verification. Record customer-facing and internal moderation reasons separately.
