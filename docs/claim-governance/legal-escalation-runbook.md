# Legal and delisting escalation runbook

This is an internal operating procedure, not legal advice. Classify incoming requests as factual/data correction, privacy concern, legal demand, source-record change, or dislike of accurate public evidence. Preserve the request and relevant source/audit record, route factual issues through record review, and escalate privacy or legal demands to designated leadership/counsel.

Accurate, publication-eligible public regulatory evidence is not removed merely because a business claims a profile or asks for deletion. Claim access never controls source evidence. Do not promise removal or legal outcomes.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE BROADER OUTREACH** for final legal positioning and response templates.
