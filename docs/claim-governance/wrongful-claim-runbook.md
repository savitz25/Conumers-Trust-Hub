# Wrongful claim incident runbook

1. Revoke the exact management grant immediately; preserve the claim and audit trail.
2. Identify the exact profile, organization, grant, claimant, and affected time window.
3. Review business-supplied edits, responses, invitations, memberships, and monitoring changes since approval.
4. Preserve evidence and audit history; never delete the event to conceal it.
5. Contact the legitimate company authority through an independently sourced channel and notify the revoked claimant neutrally.
6. Remove unauthorized team access using normal audited controls. Public regulatory evidence remains unchanged.
7. Decide whether business-supplied public content needs rollback or moderation; escalate privacy/legal concerns.
8. Require fresh authority verification for a replacement claimant and document closure rationale.
